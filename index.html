How I test a feature

Tools change. This is the order I think in, whatever the stack.

Know who pays for it

First I learn who uses the feature and why it matters to the business. That tells me where a failure would hurt most.

Rank the risks

I test the paths that touch money, trust or data first. An edge case on a settings page can wait.

Test every layer

A green screen isn't proof. I check what the UI shows, what the API returns and what actually lands in the database.

Automate what repeats

Regression goes into Playwright and CI, so people spend their time on new risks.

AI drafts. I decide.

I work with AI every day, the way a good tester works with a fast junior: it does the heavy lifting, and I check everything it gives me. Every suggestion is a hypothesis I test before I trust it.
